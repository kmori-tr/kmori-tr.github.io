About

I am a PhD Candidate in Transportation Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by Dr. Kara Kockelman. My research focuses on transportation systems, with an emphasis on shared autonomous vehicles, public transit, congestion pricing, travel demand modeling, and large-scale agent-based simulation.
