About

I am a PhD student in Civil Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by Dr. Kara Kockelman. My research focuses on transportation systems, with an emphasis on shared autonomous vehicles, congestion pricing, travel demand modeling, and large-scale agent-based simulation.

I hold an M.Eng. in Civil Engineering from Nagoya University (Global Environmental Leaders Program) and a B.S. in Civil Engineering (minor in Mathematics), summa cum laude, from Lafayette College.

Research Interests

  • Shared autonomous vehicles and fleet operations
  • Congestion pricing (delay-, distance-, and cordon-based)
  • Travel demand modeling and seasonal variation
  • Agent-based transportation simulation (POLARIS, SimMobility)
  • Transit and autonomous taxi services
